# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Reggie (a RevOps and marketing freelancer), one collaborator per client project, and the client
themselves. Reggie plans and bills from a laptop between calls; the collaborator drops in to add
and reschedule tasks on the one project they share; the client opens a link, usually from email,
to see where their project stands. Everyone is mid-task when they arrive: the job is to see the
plan, move a date, and get out.

## Product Purpose

Bough is Reggie's own freelance operations app: clients, projects, tasks, time, invoices,
contracts. The timeline is where a project is planned and shown to the client. Success is a
project plan that reads at a glance, can be changed by dragging, prints cleanly for a client,
and never exposes one client's data to another.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, professional, exact. A well-made tool that disappears into the work. The Bough serif
wordmark and sage green are the only brand marks; everything else is plain, dense, and calm.
Reference: Linear's density and restraint; Milestones PM+ (Salesforce) for the project →
milestone → task structure and its printable Gantt.

## Anti-references

The generic AI dashboard: stock-photo hero banners with italic quotes, "insight" cards with an
icon and a sentence, pill-shaped everything, gradient buttons, tiny uppercase tracked labels
above every block, cream cards inside cream pages. Also the opposite failure: a raw,
unstyled grid that looks like a spreadsheet screenshot.

## Design Principles

- The data is the interface. Chart first; chrome only where it does a job.
- Show a plan the way a project manager would draw it: milestones, tasks under them, dates
  across the top, today marked.
- Every affordance is standard. Buttons look like buttons, selects like selects, drag handles
  where bars are draggable and nowhere else.
- Client-safe by construction. Anything shared outward is read-only and prints as shown.
- Density over decoration. If an element is not helping someone read or change the plan,
  remove it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, visible focus, full keyboard operation for everything except
pointer drag (which always has a form-based alternative), status never by colour alone where
it matters (label or count alongside), and reduced-motion respected.
